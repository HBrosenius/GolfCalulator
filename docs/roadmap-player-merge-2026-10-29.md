# Duplicate-player merge — 29 October 2026

Available under **Spelare → Slå samman dubbletter**. Choose the duplicate and the profile to keep, then preview affected scorecards, local tour rosters, saved groups, personal goals and local shared-tour links before applying the change.

Only local player references change. The kept profile retains its name, photo and current handicap. Historical names, handicap inputs, gross scores, points and optional statistics stay unchanged. Server tour member identities, scoring permissions and credentials are never merged. If both profiles occur on one scorecard/group, a legacy name is ambiguous, a shared tour maps them to different members, or source-linked drafts/trash still exist, the preview explains the conflict and refuses the change. Resolve source drafts/trash before merging. Close a related open historical scorecard with **Ny runda** first.

The operation is written atomically with the affected account collections, local tour links and undo evidence. The archived source profile and before/after records form a durable merge record. A deletion tombstone and identity redirect prevent stale backups or device copies from resurrecting the duplicate. Cloud normalization applies the same redirects. After an account has merges, older clients must update before uploading snapshots; their local scoring data remains on the device. Account career statistics resolve an originally linked source profile through the redirect without changing the account identity.

Undo restores the archived source profile and the affected pre-merge records, retaining the deletion/operation evidence. It refuses to overwrite records edited or removed after merging, dependent later merges, or changed tour links. New rounds created after merging remain with the kept profile. Local tour-link undo evidence stays on the originating device and contains no scoring credentials; when needed, undo must run there. An explicit deletion after undo is respected. Quota checks and storage rollback protect against partially applied changes.

Merge history syncs with the account and is included with player exports. Full backups carry undo evidence; selective backups omit before/after records so unchecked score/group/tour collections do not leak through undo metadata. Selective backups retain redirects but may not support undo on a fresh device. Imported merge logs are combined with existing evidence, and records are normalized before restoration. Source-linked drafts from another device are normalized when resumed; conflicting duplicate seats are refused without replacing their saved data.

The first version retains up to 100 operations and uses conservative undo checks. It does not decide which of two different scores is correct or combine authoritative tour participants. These conflicts require explicit review rather than automatic score selection.

Verification covers identity-only rewrites, historical score preservation, same-card/link conflicts, stale previews, safe undo, edited-record refusal, tombstones, old-client rejection, cloud normalization, account career statistics, account isolation, full/partial backups, quota rollback and browser behavior on all three configured projects.
